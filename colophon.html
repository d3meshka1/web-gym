Technical Implementation & Status

This website is currently under development by student group SE-2533 for the underground gym Iron Dungeon Gym in Astana. The project is implemented exclusively in pure semantic HTML5 without using Cascading Style Sheets (CSS), JavaScript, frameworks, or site builders, in strict compliance with the assignment requirements.

Team Responsibilities & Current Progress

Our team consists of two members actively working on the site structure:

  • Student 1: Responsible for the main page layout, contact section, and initial site architecture (pages in progress).
  • Student 2: Responsible for the group schedule page (schedule.html) and training classes descriptions (classes.html — currently being drafted).
