Underground Infrastructure & Equipment

Iron Dungeon Gym is equipped with professional, heavy-duty gear built to withstand extreme loads. Our facility is partitioned into specialized zones designed for powerlifting, bodybuilding, and functional conditioning.

Heavy Lifting & Power Racks

We feature competition-grade steel power cages equipped with heavy safety pins, pull-up bars, and anchored platform bases to ensure maximum stability during heavy squats and bench presses.

Free Weights & Calibrated Plates

Our inventory includes an extensive selection of cast iron and calibrated steel plates, rubber-coated bumper plates, and a complete dumbbell rack ranging from 5 kg to 60 kg.

  • Calibrated steel plates (IWF standard tolerances)
    • Competition discs (Red, Blue, Yellow, Green)
    • Fractional plates for micro-loading
  • Urethane dumbbells with solid steel handles
  • Straight and EZ-curl Olympic barbell sets

Equipment Capacity & Zone Comparison

A comparative analysis of machine and free-weight density across our facility compared to standard commercial gym chains:

  • Iron Dungeon Main Power Sector (Highest Heavy Iron Density)
    • 6 competition-grade steel power cages (surpassing standard commercial layouts)
    • 4 professional deadlift platforms with reinforced rubber flooring
    • Over 1.5 tons of calibrated steel plates and iron discs
  • Functional Conditioning & Accessory Zone
    • Heavy-duty cable crossover machines (2 multi-stack stations)
    • High-traction turf tracks for prowler and sled pushes
    • Fewer electronic cardio machines, focusing instead on raw functional endurance gear (battle ropes, climbing ropes, concept rowers)
  • Comparison vs. Standard Commercial Chain Gyms
    • Commercial Gyms:
      • Higher count of motorized treadmills and stationary exercise bikes
      • Lighter dumbbell ranges (stopping mostly at 35–40 kg)
    • Iron Dungeon Gym:
      • Significantly more heavy compound equipment and specialized power racks
      • Dumbbell racks extending up to 60 kg for advanced hypertrophy
      • Greater concentration of free-weight stations over isolated machine slots

Accessory & Conditioning Hardware

To supplement compound movements, the facility provides various specialized attachment tools and functional fitness hardware.

  1. Heavy-duty cable crossover machines
  2. Glute-ham raise (GHR) benches
  3. Prowler sleds and high-traction turf tracks
  4. Climbing ropes and heavy battle ropes

Equipment Maintenance Protocol

All bars and knurling are inspected and brushed weekly. As a rule of the gym, Rack your weights immediately after use or face the penalty.
