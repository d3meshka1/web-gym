About Branches

Today, the Underground Gym chain comprises 11 state-of-the-art locations covering the city’s key districts—Esil, Almaty, Saryarka, and Baikonur. All locations are united by a single concept of uncompromising quality: guests can expect a consistently high standard of premium professional equipment, a uniform pricing policy, and consistent service standards, allowing them to work out at any location in the network with a single membership.
