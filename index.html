About the Club

Underground Gym is a fitness club chain in Astana. We opened our doors to provide people with a simple and honest space for training: no pushy consultants, no hidden fees, and no unnecessary showiness. We have modern equipment, spacious gyms, and a professional team of trainers ready to support you at every stage of your fitness journey.

Results come from consistency, not trendy diets or one-time intensive workouts. That is why our club offers a flexible membership system and open group classes. Every branch has a free weights area, cardio zone, changing rooms with showers, and a stretching area where you can effectively finish any workout.

We are located in different districts of the city, making it convenient to train close to your home or office. Join Underground Gym and become part of our friendly sports community of people who genuinely care about their body, strength, and health every day.
